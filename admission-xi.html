5 Previous school (all compulsory)

Write the last school attended. For Class XI this is the school where Madhyamik was passed.

6 Bank account for scholarships (optional)

Student's own / joint account with parent. Used only for scholarship & Kanyashree payments.

8 Madhyamik (Class X) result

Enter marks exactly as in the Madhyamik mark-sheet. Subject eligibility for Class XI is checked from these marks (minimum 35 where required).

9 Subjects for Class XI

Minimum 5 subjects: 2 languages (automatic) + 3 electives. 1 optional elective may be added. Subjects needing a pass in Madhyamik stay disabled until the marks above are entered and are at least 35. Computer Application (COMA) has no marks requirement; Science students may take one of COMA or COMS.

10 Photo & declaration

Student's signature (optional upload)

  • Sign on plain white paper with a black ink ball pen.
  • Write your full signature as you normally sign — not in block (capital) letters.
  • Take a clear, well-lit photo or scan, crop close to the signature, then upload it here.

Bring these originals when called for verification:

  • Birth certificate / age proof
  • Transfer Certificate & last mark-sheet / progress report, Madhyamik admit card & mark-sheet
  • Caste certificate (if SC / ST / OBC)
  • Aadhaar of student & parent, passport-size photos (2)
  • Bank passbook copy (if account given), Kanyashree / disability documents (if applicable)
