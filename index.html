Share Data Collection Link

Send this link to staff. They fill the form online and it reaches your Google Sheet; you then import it with Fetch Submissions.

Guide for staff (included in the message)

  1. Open the link on a mobile or computer with internet.
  2. Fill School Information and Employee Details. Only Name of the Staff and Number of previous schools are compulsory.
  3. Fill education and service rows. Boxes that do not apply turn grey automatically.
  4. Enter Commutation as a whole number (for 40% type 40, not 0.4).
  5. Tap Submit Details once and wait for the green tick with a Reference number. Take a screenshot of it.
  6. If an error shows, your entries are kept. Check internet and tap Submit again.

Guide for you (office)

  1. Log in, click 📥 Fetch Submissions and then Refresh.
  2. Tick the records to bring in (ones marked already in portal are left unticked) and click Import Selected.
  3. Open each imported record, check the data, add anything missing and click Save Record.
  4. Go to 3. Documents / Print to print the pension papers.
  5. Imported rows are marked IMPORTED in the Sheet, so they are not imported twice.

Login to Edit

Sign in to add, edit, save, delete, or import pensioner records. Anyone can still view and print without logging in.
